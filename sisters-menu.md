## Naming locks

- Signature drink is **Bumble Coffee**, never "Bubble Coffee".
- Sweet Sisters' and Bitter Sisters' are never translated into Turkish.
- Turkish craft beer is **Butik**, never "Zanaat".
- Single variety herbal tea is **Aromalı Bitki Çayı**.
- "Ask a Sister for specifics", never "Ask your server".
- No em dashes or any other dashes in customer-facing copy.
- illy statement: All cups are made with illy. Your unique experience at Sisters' begins with our bean choice.

## Notes

- Redbull Red Margarita is 480 on the main menu and 580 on the cocktail sheet; 580 used here.
